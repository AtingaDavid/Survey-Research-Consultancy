Executive summary

Fieldwork is 71% complete and about two days behind the needed pace. HQ rejection (3.7%, 47 interviews) is below the 5% threshold, and 93.5% of interviews have GPS. Risk is concentrated in two of 15 active interviewers.

Progress

1,284 of 1,800 interviews are complete, carried out by 15 interviewers. The current pace is 38.6 a day against 43 needed. The projected finish is about 6 Jun, against a 4 Jun target.

Data quality

6.9% of interviews (89) have more than 5 invalid answers; the mean is 2.3 invalid answers per interview across all completed interviews. Mean duration is 28.4 min (median 25.9). 83 interviews lack GPS. 108 interviews (8.4%) started between 20:00 and 05:00, and 14 of these are flagged for review.

Risk

INT-04 and INT-07 score 5 (high). 48 interviews (3.7% of completed interviews) score 7 or more (critical). The indicators prioritise back-checks and are not evidence of misconduct.

Recommended actions

  1. Back-check the INT-04 and INT-07 workloads and the 48 interviews scoring 7 or more.
  2. Follow up the 83 interviews without GPS and the 14 flagged night interviews.
  3. Close the gap of about 4 interviews a day, or move the target date.

Methods, Thresholds & Limitations
Data and purpose
The checks use the Survey Solutions paradata export (answer, validation and status events with timestamps and user IDs), interview status history and GPS question values. The purpose is to rank interviews and interviewers for review, so limited back-check and supervision effort goes where it matters most. This demo report uses simulated data with the same structure.
Flag definitions
FlagRule
Too fast / too slowInterview duration under 10 min or over 90 min
High-error interviewMore than 5 invalid answers (validation errors) in one interview. Invalid answers are counted from QuestionDeclaredInvalid events, and the mean of 2.3 is per completed interview
Excessive editsMore than 50 answer edits in one interview
Missing / poor GPSNo coordinates captured, or accuracy worse than 50 m
Night start / night_int flagNight start: began between 20:00 and 05:00 (108 interviews, 8.4%). The night_int flag, used in the risk score, marks the 14 night starts flagged for review
StraightliningIdentical answers across a question battery
Duplicate GPSSame coordinates in several interviews by one interviewer
RejectedRejected by HQ (or supervisor) after review
Thresholds are starting values. Calibrate them in the pilot to the questionnaire length and field conditions before fieldwork.
How to use the results
  1. Review the flagged interviews in HQ.
  2. Back-check the highest-priority interviews, plus a random sample of the other interviewers' work, by phone or re-visit.
  3. Give feedback or retraining where problems are confirmed.
  4. Reject, re-interview or drop interviews only after confirmation.
Limitations
A flag shows an unusual pattern, not misconduct: long or short interviews, missing GPS and night work often have legitimate causes. Paradata cannot confirm that the respondent was contacted. Several flags raise the chance of false positives, and interviewer-level scores rest on small samples. Interviewer effects are summarised with the ICC alongside the ANOVA.
Practice reference
The approach follows the high-frequency checks and back-check practice used in field surveys, for example IPA's high-frequency checks (ipacheck) and DIME Analytics' Development Research in Practice, applied to Survey Solutions paradata.
