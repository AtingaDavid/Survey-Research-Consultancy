Platform Advantages

Why Survey Solutions?

Survey Solutions is a free CAPI/CAWI/CATI platform developed by the World Bank and co-financed by FAO and the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation. Deployed in 94+ countries by national statistical offices, governments, and research institutions, it is the platform of choice where data quality and total cost-efficiency cannot both be compromised.

🌐
CAWI: Self-Administered Online Interviews
Web-mode (CAWI) lets respondents complete questionnaires on any device via a secure URL, suited to Key Informant Interviews, expert surveys, and remote data collection. Auto email reminders keep response rates high without manual follow-up, and respondents can resume a partially completed interview at any time, even generating their own resume-link to pick up on a different device, no extra server setup required.
📡
GPS, GIS & Offline Mapping
Capture precise GPS coordinates, land areas, and boundaries using the built-in geography question type. Interviewers can find sampling points offline using high-resolution satellite imagery, and apply geofencing to enforce interview location compliance, even with no mobile signal.
✅
Validation and Skip Logic
Write validation rules and skip logic using .NET expressions, macros, and lookup tables. Errors are flagged during the interview, which cuts back-office data cleaning. Supervisor rejection and commenting workflows enforce multi-level quality control.
🔒
No Software Cost, World Bank Backing, Any Scale
Developed and maintained by the World Bank, free at any scale, with no licensing fee, no subscription tier, and no feature gates. It scales from small KII studies to national census operations, with user role management (HQ admin → Supervisor → Interviewer) and full REST API access for system integration. Commercial alternatives like SurveyCTO or CommCare typically start at USD 250–1,000+ per month for equivalent functionality, Survey Solutions carries zero software cost at any scale.
📤
Export-Ready for Any Analysis Software
Export clean, labelled datasets directly in STATA (.dta), SPSS (.sav), CSV, or tab-delimited formats, with all question labels, value labels, and metadata intact. Export paradata alongside responses for interview process analysis.
🧾
Automatic paradata for quality checks
Every interview automatically generates a full event log, recording who entered each answer, when, in what order, and how many times it was edited. No setup required. Used for interviewer performance scoring, audit-trail documentation, and real-time field quality control. No competing free platform produces structured paradata; it is a paid feature in SurveyCTO and unavailable in KoBoToolbox and ODK.
👥
Role-Structured Field Management
A four-tier user hierarchy, Administrator, Headquarters, Supervisor, Interviewer, each with its own app and defined permissions. Supervisors review and reject interviews with question-level comments. HQ monitors the full sample in real time. Assignments are created individually or in batch and pushed to supervisor and interviewer dashboards automatically, at any scale, with full audit trail.
Collection Modes Supported

One Platform,
Every Interview Mode

Survey Solutions supports mixed-mode data collection, allowing the same questionnaire instrument to be deployed across multiple channels in the same survey round, with CAPI, CAWI, and CATI responses merging automatically into a single export file, ready for analysis.

CAPI
Computer-Assisted Personal Interview Source: Enumerator-led on Android tablets or smartphones, with full offline capability. Audio audit, barcode, image, and sensor capture supported.
CAWI
Computer-Assisted Web Interview Source: Respondent self-completes via browser. Suited to KIIs, expert panels, and remote stakeholders. Auto email reminders and resume-after-start capability built in.
CATI
Computer-Assisted Telephone Interview Source: Enumerator-administered over phone. The platform records call metadata alongside responses for quality verification.
GPS
Geospatial Data Capture Source: Points, polygons, polylines, and boundaries. Offline satellite imagery for finding sampling points. Geofencing to enforce sampling protocols in the field.

01 Questionnaire Design
Survey Solutions enables designers to validate answers and direct interview flow with the power of .NET; use macros, calculated variables, and lookup tables to build complex validation logic. Complex questionnaires can be developed in several languages.
Source: World Bank, mysurvey.solutions
  • Rich question typology: Supports text, numeric, date/time, single- and multi-select, GPS, barcode, photo, audio, roster (repeating group), and linked/cascading questions, natively, with no plugins required.
  • .NET logic engine: Skip patterns, enabling conditions, and validation rules are written in C# expressions, more flexible than the XLSForm syntax used by ODK, KoBoToolbox, and SurveyCTO. Macro definitions, calculated variables, and lookup tables support algorithmic data validation.
  • Answer piping and cascading questions: Collected responses drive subsequent question text and filter answer options dynamically.
  • Linked and linked-combobox questions: Questions can link to responses collected earlier in the same interview or pre-loaded in the assignment. This supports panel follow-up without custom scripting.
  • Multilingual questionnaire support: Full multi-language design is native; language can be switched at runtime.
  • Reusable category libraries: Answer lists (crop types, administrative units, etc.) are defined once and reused across any question in the instrument.
  • Collaborative cloud design: Multiple team members work on the same questionnaire simultaneously from any browser, without accessing client server infrastructure.
  • Cover page generation: System-generated cover pages capture interviewer identity and assignment metadata before the substantive interview begins.

02 Questionnaire Testing
  • Zero-infrastructure testing: The built-in Tester runs in the browser on the public Designer site, no server, no tablet, no IT setup required before logic verification begins.
  • Pilot-to-production continuity: The same questionnaire file used in testing is imported directly to the HQ server; no reformatting or version divergence.
  • Interview diagnostics file: Export archives include a file containing interview duration, error count, comment count, and unanswered questions per interview, objective metrics for pilot quality assessment.
  • Mode-specific testing: The same instrument can be tested in CAPI, CAWI, and CATI modes, allowing mixed-mode pilots to assess mode effects before full deployment.

05 Paradata for quality control
🧾 Recorded automatically for every event, at no extra cost.
Every survey collected in Survey Solutions is supplemented with a paradata file, produced automatically, with no setup required from the questionnaire designer or HQ team. The paradata records every event: answer entered, answer changed, interview paused/resumed, approved or rejected, comment added, each attributed to a named user with a UTC timestamp and device time-zone offset.
  • Interviewer performance monitoring: Duration per question, total interview duration, and back-editing patterns flag enumerators who may be rushing, fabricating, or deviating from protocol.
  • Composite quality risk scoring: Paradata can be processed in R, Stata, or Python to produce risk scores per interviewer, use it for targeted back-checks and retraining.
  • Audit-ready documentation: A formal, exportable audit log satisfying donor data quality assurance requirements without additional tooling.
  • No competing free platform provides this: KoBoToolbox and ODK produce no structured paradata. SurveyCTO offers audio audit as a paid feature. Survey Solutions includes full event-level paradata as a standard, automatic output, at zero software cost.
